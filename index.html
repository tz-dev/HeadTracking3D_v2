Virtual room / monitor

Room depth sets the virtual room’s fixed offset. In Room mode, depth-related perspective distortion is deliberately reduced: immersion and Z movement mostly move your viewpoint through the world. Window and Hybrid modes retain their original behavior. In Room mode, R recalibrates distance and neutral gaze. Position and head orientation are camera-relative: image center + facing forward = neutral. Starting off-center stays off-center. Typical viewing distance: 50–75 cm.
Monitor z=0 · Room +3.00 · Travel +0.00

Sensitivity

Gaze is measured from iris positions relative to the eye boundaries rather than noisy blendshapes. Head yaw/pitch and gaze X/Y affect Room, Hybrid and Look-around modes. Classic Window mode keeps its viewing direction fixed. In Room mode, the webcam image center is the position/orientation origin; R only recalibrates gaze and distance. Other modes retain their previous calibration behavior.
Room: actual X/Y tracking, damped projection Z, virtual forward/back travel and moderated head rotation. Window: classic off-axis projection with a fixed viewing direction. Use R to set the head-neutral pose in non-Room modes. Pause object animations to compare screenshots.
